<h2 align="center">🚀 About Me</h2>

I'm a BSc Hons Mathematics graduate (IIT-JAM qualified) and tech enthusiast passionate about problem-solving and software development. My curiosity drives me to explore complex computational problems, from researching and mathematically modeling metaheuristic optimization algorithms to developing frameworks for 3D UAV Path Planning. 

Alongside my analytical research, I love building robust software. I actively work on full-stack development and AI integrations, leveraging technologies like Python, Flask, Node.js, and Express to build scalable backend architectures and impactful applications. Whether I'm building a mobile app backend, creating predictive models, or fine-tuning algorithms, I am always eager to learn, grow, and build solutions that make a real-world impact.
